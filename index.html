Esta edición

Edición familiar Brito Devoto: conversión del documento original a GEDCOM 5.5.1 y este visor. La obra de Graeme Wall estuvo publicada en abierto entre 2004 y 2019; al desaparecer su sitio, esta edición la vuelve a poner en línea.

Por respeto a quienes siguen vivos, de las personas sin defunción registrada y nacidas después de 1930 se muestra sólo el nombre: ni fechas, ni lugares, ni notas.

Se muestran los datos tal como figuraban en la publicación original. Si aparecés en este árbol y querés que se quiten o corrijan tus datos, escribí a santiagobrito@gmail.com y lo hacemos.

Original source

The data comes from Descendants of Hugh Robson, the genealogical research of Graeme Wall, version 10z, 4 May 2015.

© Graeme Wall, 2004–2014.

It was published at http://www.greywall.demon.co.uk/genealogy/robson.html. That site is gone: it disappeared when Demon Internet, the British provider hosting it, shut down. Copies survive at the Internet Archive.

This edition

A family edition by the Brito Devoto family: the original document converted to GEDCOM 5.5.1, plus this viewer. Graeme Wall's work was openly published between 2004 and 2019; with his site gone, this edition puts it back online.

Out of respect for the living, people with no recorded death who were born after 1930 are shown by name only: no dates, no places, no notes.

The data is shown as it appeared in the original publication. If you appear in this tree and would like your details removed or corrected, write to santiagobrito@gmail.com and we will take care of it.
